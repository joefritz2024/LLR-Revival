# LLR-Revival 8.0 – Build 4

**Your Logos library, your sermon and your slides – in one place.**

LLR-Revival turns your Logos/Verbum library into a sermon workshop: search your books in seconds, build your outline (with optional AI help), write your sermon and turn it into slides – all on your own computer.

## ✨ What's new in Build 4

LLR-Revival 8.0 Build 4 (October 1, 2026) builds on Build 1 with more accurate book categories, five new diagram styles and a reworked timeline. Slides are now fully free-form and export to PowerPoint, saving and opening work reliably, and the app tells you when an update is available. Setup installations now work fully in English, French and Spanish.

---

## 🌟 Highlights of version 8.0

- **Presentations** – sermon slides for Bible verses, quotes, comparisons and titles, straight from your AI outline.
- **Diagrams** – tree, mind map, timeline, pyramid, scales, honeycomb, mountain path or orbits.
- **Word Study** – for any word in any language, not only biblical Hebrew and Greek.
- **Document Analysis** – compact summaries and main statements from whole books and long texts; the Document Scanner does the same for shorter texts.
- **Focus Mode** – distraction-free writing.
- **Copy, cut and paste** as visible buttons in the Text Workshop, in Presentations and in the text sidebar.

> **Note:** Opening Logos links directly is no longer possible automatically. Use the copy-and-paste function you already know from the Sermon Workshop instead – it now works everywhere.
