# LLR-Revival v8.0 NovaPro

**Compatible with Logos Bible Software® and Verbum®**

A fully browser-based library explorer for Logos/Verbum — developed by Pastor Hermann Fritz (FeG Horb am Neckar, Germany) as a revival and major extension of Steve Clark's original Logos Library Reporter (2015).

## What is LLR-Revival?

LLR-Revival reads your local Logos/Verbum database and presents all your books in a clear, feature-rich web interface — running directly in the browser, locally, without any additional app installation. No cloud dependency, no subscription, no data ever leaves your computer unless you explicitly choose to sync it.

Over time it has grown far beyond a library browser into a complete study and sermon-preparation workbench for pastors, students, and researchers.

## Key Features

### 📚 Library Explorer
- Automatic categorization of 22,000+ books into 40+ theological categories
- Fast search by title, author, category, and year
- Advanced search with Logos-tag filtering (`myTag:TagName`)
- "Related resources" discovery from any book
- Full offline PDF/cover caching for a fast, native-feeling browsing experience

### ✍️ Sermon Workshop
- Full sermon editor with page-accurate "desktop" layout, autosave, and timer
- **Sermon series generator**: suggest a full preaching series from a Bible passage or theme, with tone and length control
- One-click "sermon tie" and small-group-question generation from any outline
- Focus mode and true fullscreen writing mode with a floating minimal toolbar
- Save-as / Open with your own folder structure — find your files again yourself
- Import from .docx/.txt, export to .docx/.txt/HTML, with footnotes/endnotes and page numbering
- 10 formatting templates across sermon, letter, and occasion categories
- Full citation support: Chicago/Turabian, SBL, APA, and MLA — auto-generated from book metadata

### 🎬 Presentations
- Build PowerPoint-ready slide decks directly from your sermon or study content
- True fullscreen slideshow playback
- Drag-and-drop slide reordering, image pool, and per-slide image placement
- Presenter-notes visibility toggle for confident delivery
- Live, aspect-ratio-correct design preview matching the final PPTX export

### 🔎 Document Analysis
- Upload and analyze PDF, DOCX, TXT, EPUB, ODT, FB2, RTF, or HTML documents
- Automatic metadata detection (title, author, publisher, year)
- Page-range extraction for PDFs, chapter-aware handling for EPUBs
- Built-in translation of extracted passages
- One-click bibliography/citation generation and export straight into your editor

### 🌳 Diagram Builder
- Turn any outline or set of ideas into a clean, presentable mind-map/diagram
- Send diagrams straight to your work projects

### ❓ Free Question
- Ask an open theological or study question and get a structured, sourced answer
- Send results straight to your editor, presentation, or a project

### 📖 Word Studies
- In-depth word study tool with results you can save, present, or file into a project

### 💬 Quote Book
- Capture and organize Bible and book quotations as you read
- Turn any quote into a presentation slide with one click

### 📁 Work Projects
- A single hub that ties every module together — 17 "send to project" bridges connect Sermon Workshop, Word Studies, Diagrams, Free Question, Document Analysis, Presentations, and more
- One click from almost anywhere in the app: "send this to a project"

### 📝 Notes
- Fast note-taking with autosave, always one click away from the navigation bar

### 🔍 Workshop Search
- Full-text search across all your own notes, projects, quotes, and entries — from one place, across the whole program

### 🕸️ Connections Overview
- A new visual map in the Help menu shows all 12 core modules and how they connect — a quick way to see everything the program can do

### 🤖 AI Features
- AI-assisted recommendations, outline generation, and content re-ranking
- Works with your own API key (Anthropic, Groq, and others) **or fully offline** via [Ollama](https://ollama.com) — no internet connection, no cloud transfer, no cost

### ☁️ Cloud Sync & Backup
- Optional device sync via a private GitHub Gist — your data, your GitHub account
- Snapshot and full backup/restore, with change tracking against your last snapshot
- Favorites import/export

### 🌍 Multi-Language
- Fully available in **German, English, French, and Spanish** — switch anytime

### 🖥️ Runs Everywhere
- **Windows**: one-click installer (Setup.exe) or a portable, no-install package
- **Mac & Linux**: simple double-click installer scripts, fully documented for Gatekeeper and first-run quirks
- **iPhone, iPad, and Android**: runs as a browser-based app — no app store needed

### 🔒 Privacy by Design
- Everything runs on your own machine against your own local Logos/Verbum database
- No account, no subscription, no tracking
- Fully free and open source
